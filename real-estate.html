Agent + Mortgage + Insurance FAQ

Can you integrate IDX / MLS listings?
Yes. Growth and Enterprise support IDX integrations (iHomefinder, Realtyna, IDX Broker). Listings pull automatically and match your site design. Compliance-friendly.
What about TCPA / RESPA / state compliance?
We use double-opt-in forms, proper disclosures, and compliant SMS patterns. For mortgage, we follow RESPA on referral practices. We'll align with your compliance officer on the audit call — not your first rodeo.
Can your AI chat speak Spanish to buyer leads?
Yes — massive advantage in SoCal. Spanish-speaking buyers are underserved. Rivven's chatbot auto-detects language and answers in Spanish. Competitors don't do this. You win bilingual leads by default.

Keep the paid leads if they work, but own your own funnel too. Rivven's site is the asset you KEEP when Zillow raises prices again. Best agents have both: paid + owned.
