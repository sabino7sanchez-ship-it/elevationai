Auto-Shop FAQ

Do you have a real auto-shop client?
Yes. Trippy Blendz (mobile detailing, paying customer). Site is live, GBP running, booking flow tested. We can share the demo and real numbers on the audit call.
Can I take bookings when I'm under a car?
Yes. The AI chatbot + SMS system handles the whole intake while you work. It asks service type, vehicle, preferred time, and drops a confirmation into your calendar. You just show up.
My customers mostly text — can you make the whole thing SMS-first?
Yes. Growth tier includes two-way SMS booking. Customer texts → AI qualifies → books → reminds. You see the conversation, no app to learn.
Does Rivven work for mobile-only (no shop address)?
Yes. Service-area businesses (SAB) have their own GBP setup (no pin, service radius). We've configured this for mobile detailing and towing specifically.
What about Spanish-speaking customers in SoCal?
Critical for auto. Most detailing + body shop customers in SoCal are bilingual. Rivven's chatbot detects language and responds in Spanish or English — your competitors don't do this.
