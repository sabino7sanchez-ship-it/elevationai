🎓 Cold Call Masterclass · Read once · Re-read between calls
▸ THE ONE TRUTH

You're not "selling" — you're filtering. 100 calls = 95 nos, 5 maybes, 1-2 yeses. Your job is to get through the 95 fast, qualify the 5, close the 2. The fear of "rejection" is a fear of the 95 — but the 95 aren't rejecting you, they're rejecting a pitch they didn't want. You can't take it personally because they don't know you yet.

LESSON 1 · ANATOMY OF FIRST 30 SECONDS
Every cold call is won or lost in the first 30 sec. Here's what's happening in the prospect's brain second-by-second.
0-3 SEC · "WHO IS THIS"
Their brain: stranger, default = annoyed. Your job: sound human, not scripted. Say their name OR your real name. Don't say "your name from your company." That's a robot. → "Hey [Name] — this is Sabino…"
3-7 SEC · "IS THIS A PITCH"
Their brain: bracing for sales. Your job: pattern-interrupt. Don't sound like a pitch. → "This isn't a pitch." OR "Full transparency — this is a cold call." Naming the elephant disarms it.
7-15 SEC · "WHAT DO THEY WANT"
Their brain: deciding whether to hang up. Your job: give them a reason that's about them, not you. → "I noticed you're owner-operated…" Specificity > pitch.
15-30 SEC · "CAN I TRUST THEM"
Their brain: permission to listen. Your job: earn it with a time-box. → "90 seconds, you say yes or no, we both move on. Cool?" They feel in control → they stay.
LESSON 2 · VOICE MECHANICS · HOW YOU SOUND MATTERS MORE THAN WHAT YOU SAY
Phone strips 93% of communication (no body language, no facial expression). All you've got = voice. Master it.
▸ PACE · slow down 20%
Anxiety = fast talker. Confidence = slow. Slow your normal speech 20%. Pause between sentences for a full second. Pauses signal control. If you sound rushed, they hang up.
▸ PITCH · drop low at end
"Late-night DJ voice." End sentences with a DOWN inflection, not UP. Up = question = needy. Down = statement = confident. Practice: "Hey [Name]" ends down, not up.
▸ SMILE · they hear it
Smile while you talk. They literally hear it in your voice. Studies show 40% higher engagement with smiling speaker. Even on a cold call where they can't see you. Smile through "no" — disarms instantly.
▸ STAND UP · changes your tone
Sit = compressed diaphragm = weak voice. Stand = open chest = full sound. Pace while you talk if you can. Cardone, Hormozi, Belfort all stand and pace.
▸ PAUSE · silence is power
After a closing question — SHUT UP. First to speak loses. They will fill silence with truth. Count 5 seconds in your head. They'll talk. They always do.
LESSON 3 · 80/20 RULE · LISTEN MORE THAN YOU TALK
Best cold callers talk 20% of the call, listen 80%. Most beginners do the opposite and lose.

Your job: ask questions, then SHUT UP. The prospect tells you exactly how to sell to them — if you let them talk.

Power questions to ask:

  • "What's your avg ticket?" → forces them to actually think about their numbers
  • "What's the missed-call problem costing you a month?" → makes them quantify their pain
  • "What would have to be true for this to be a yes today?" → pulls the real objection out
  • "Walk me through your last bad day — phone-wise." → gets them venting → reveals truth
  • "Out of curiosity, what do you do when the phone rings while you're on a job?" → their answer reveals their pain

Don't: respond instantly after they answer. Pause 2 seconds. Then mirror their last 2-3 words back to them. They'll keep talking.

Mirror example:
Them: "Yeah, we lose like 5-10 calls a week."
You: "...5-10 calls a week."
Them: "...yeah, and probably half of those are emergencies that go to the next plumber..." ← gold

LESSON 4 · REAL-TIME DECISION TREE · WHAT TO SAY WHEN THEY SAY X
Memorize 4 branches. Every cold call splits into one of these. You have a response for each.
BRANCH A · "WHO IS THIS?" / "YEAH?" (neutral)
= 60% of pickups. They're cautious but listening. → Go straight into opener #2 (permission timebox). Don't apologize, don't over-explain. "Hey — quick, you the owner? Cool. 90 seconds…"
BRANCH B · "I'M BUSY" / "BAD TIME"
= 25% of pickups. They're testing you. → Use that AGAINST them: "I figured — that's why I called. You're busy because you can't get to the phone. 30 seconds tonight at 7?" Bonus: drop a callback time and HONOR it. Builds trust.
BRANCH C · "NOT INTERESTED" / "TAKE ME OFF YOUR LIST"
= 10% of pickups. Annoyed. → Don't fight. Use the 10-second value drop: "Fair, completely get it. 10 seconds and I'm gone — I looked at your shop, you're [specific detail], I build AI for guys like you. If interesting, text me back tomorrow. If not, gone." 1 in 20 texts back 2 weeks later.
BRANCH D · "WHO IS THIS?" (aggressive)
= 5% of pickups. Pissed off. → Soft + specific: "Sabino with RIVVEN, AI agency in LA. Cold call, I'll be quick — 30 seconds, you decide." Confidence + brevity defuses. If they keep yelling → exit warmly: "All good, I'll leave you alone." Hang up. Move on.
LESSON 5 · 7 TELLS · HOW TO READ ENGAGEMENT ON THE PHONE
You can't see them, but you can hear what they're doing. Learn the audio cues.
🟢 They ask a follow-up question → engaged, push forward
🟢 They name a number ("yeah I lose maybe 5 a week") → in math mode = buying signal
🟢 They sigh and say "yeah…" → recognition, pain acknowledged
🟡 Long silence after your question → thinking, not bored. WAIT IT OUT.
🟡 "Hmm" / "interesting" → considering, not committed yet. Drop more proof.
🟠 Short answers, no questions back → low engagement, escalate value or exit
🔴 Background noise growing (TV, voices, banging) → distracted, they're not present. Reschedule: "sounds like you're in it — when's a better 5 min?"
LESSON 6 · 5 DAILY DRILLS · SHARPEN BEFORE YOU CALL
15 minutes/morning. The same drills Cardone, Belfort, Voss did before they were legends.
1 · Record yourself reading opener #2 verbatim 5x. Play back. Do you sound rushed? Robotic? Adjust pace + smile.
2 · Cold-call yourself (literally — leave yourself a voicemail using the opener). Listen to it. Would YOU take that call?
3 · Objection drill — read each of 16 objections out loud, then say the Label + Reframe out loud from memory. Repeat 3x daily until reflex.
4 · "What would have to be true" drill — ask yourself 5 things you've been on the fence about, answer with this question. Trains the close mindset.
5 · The 5-second hold — practice silent pauses. Read a question, count to 5 silently. Build the muscle to NOT fill silence.
LESSON 7 · RECOVERY PATTERNS · WHEN YOU SCREW UP
Everyone fumbles. The pros recover in 1 sentence. Here's how.
You stumbled on the opener: "Hold up — let me start over. Cold call, 30 seconds. Cool?" → smile through it. They respect the recovery.
You said something wrong: "Actually — scratch that. What I meant was…" → owning the mistake = trust.
You went silent / brain-froze: "[Name] — you still with me? OK, one second…" → buy time, then restart with opener #3 transparent cold.
You over-talked: Just stop mid-sentence. "…sorry, talking too much. Your turn — what do you think?" → instant reset.
They asked a question you can't answer: "Honest answer — I don't know. Let me find out and text you tonight. What's the best number?" → honesty crushes BS.
They got hostile: Soften immediately: "All good. I hear you. I'll let you go." Hang up warmly. Don't argue. 1 in 50 calls back apologizing.
LESSON 8 · THE CLOSER MINDSET · YOU FILTER, NOT SELL
The mental frame that separates pros from amateurs.

Amateur frame: "I hope they buy. I need this. They might say no."
Pro frame: "I'm filtering for fit. If they're a yes, great. If they're a no, next one. I have 7 more dials in the queue."

Why it works: needy energy is contagious. Confident detached energy is also contagious. Prospects buy from people who don't need to sell. The moment you stop needing the deal, you start closing more deals.

Practice line — say out loud before every call: "I don't need this deal. They either fit or they don't. I have 100 more to call."

Assume the sale: default mental state = "this is going to close." Talk like it. "What email goes on the invoice?" not "would you like to maybe purchase?" See CLOSE-03 in CLOSES tab.

⚡ THE 4-STEP OBJECTION PATTERN
Every objection — no exceptions — gets the same 4-step response. Never argue, never defend. Drill this until it's reflex.
1 · LABEL (disarm)
"Sounds like the price feels heavy."
2 · REFRAME (redirect)
"You're missing $X/mo right now. The system isn't a cost, it's plugging a leak."
3 · EVIDENCE (prove)
"One shop missed $18K/mo. We recovered $11K of it in 60 days. Setup paid back in 27."
4 · MICRO-CLOSE (re-take the floor)
"If I show the bleeding stops in 30 days, are we good to move?" — then SILENCE. First to speak loses.

6 Battle-Tested Closer Patterns · Stack 2 Per Call
⚠ CLOSER RULES Pick ONE closer to land the deal. If they hesitate, use a second. Never stack 3+ in one breath. After every closer = SILENCE. First to speak loses (Sales Bible Part A Rule 5.1).
CLOSER #1 · CARDONE "WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE"
"What would have to be true for this to be a yes today?"
Pries out the real objection. Use when they say "let me think about it." Forces them to articulate what's blocking them.
CLOSER #2 · VOSS "NO-ORIENTED" QUESTION
"Is it ridiculous to think we could get this set up by Friday?"

"Are you against the idea of plugging that $[their bleed]/mo leak?"
"No" feels safe → they answer truthfully. A forced "No" actually means yes. Use when energy is low.
CLOSER #3 · TWO-OPTION (default)
"Two paths. Zoom Tuesday 2 PM, I walk you through it. Or — lock today, I waive the $500 deploy fee, we're live Friday. Which?"
Either-or removes "no" as an option. Both choices = a yes. Default close for every call.
CLOSER #4 · BELFORT MINI-YES LADDER (assumption stack)
"So you'd agree the missed-call problem is real, right?" [yes] ··· "And you'd agree $[mid-monthly] against $[their bleed] is no-brainer math?" [yes] ··· "Cool — what email gets the agreement?"
Stack 2-3 micro-yeses → assume the big yes. Use when they've engaged but haven't committed.
CLOSER #5 · REVERSE / PERMISSION CLOSE
"Would it be ok if I sent over the agreement right now? You read it tonight, hit reply with a question, we're live Friday."
Asks permission → they feel in control → they say yes. Low-pressure close for skeptical prospects.
CLOSER #6 · 1-2 PUNCH (objection extinguisher)
"I get it — you want to think about it. What if I sent the agreement, you read it tonight with [partner], if you have questions you text me, and if it's a yes you reply with the address that goes on the invoice? Worst case you ghost me, no harm. Sound fair?"
Acknowledges hesitation + offers low-friction path forward + "sound fair" = yes pull. Use after they say "let me think" twice.
▸ MAGIC PHRASES — DROP ANYWHERE
• Disarm: "Out of curiosity…"
• Re-engage: "Just so I understand…"
• Force commitment: "If I could solve [X] for you today, would you be open to moving forward?"
• Pull objection out: "What would stop you from saying yes right now?"
• Re-take floor: "Help me understand…"
• Pivot from price: "It's not about cost — it's about return. Walk me through your last month."
• Pressure release: "There's no right answer here — just tell me what you're thinking."
• Lock yes: "Sounds like you've already decided. Should we get started?"
