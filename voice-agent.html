Does it actually sound human?

Yes. We use the latest voice synthesis tech with natural speech patterns, pauses, and inflection. Most callers cannot tell the difference.

How does bilingual support work?

The AI detects the caller's language within the first few seconds and switches automatically. It is fully fluent in both English and Spanish.

Google Calendar, Calendly, Acuity, GoHighLevel, and most CRM platforms. We also support custom integrations on the Enterprise plan.

A full-time receptionist costs $2,500-$4,000/month, works 8 hours, takes breaks, and calls in sick. The AI works 24/7/365, never has a bad day, and is priced to your call volume — typically a fraction of a single human salary at every band.
