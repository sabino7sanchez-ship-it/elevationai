Yes. All partner tiers include full white-label rights. Your clients will never see the RIVVEN name. Replace logos, colors, domains, and footer credits with your own branding.
What kind of support do I get? +
Starter partners get email support (48hr response). Growth partners get priority email (24hr). Enterprise partners get a dedicated partner manager, quarterly strategy calls, and white-glove onboarding.
Is there exclusivity? Can competitors use it too? +
The Starter and Growth tiers are non-exclusive. Enterprise partners can negotiate geographic or industry exclusivity as part of their partnership agreement.
How often do you release new tools and templates? +
We ship new templates and tools monthly. All partners on Growth and Enterprise tiers receive every update automatically at no additional cost.
What's the refund policy? +
We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee on all tiers. If you're not satisfied, email us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. No questions asked.
How many clients can I serve? +
Unlimited. There are no per-client fees or seat limits. Serve 1 client or 100 clients on the same plan. You keep 100% of every dollar you charge.
