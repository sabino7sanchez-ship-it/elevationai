How is pricing tailored to my business?
We band you by your monthly gross + team size + call volume — then quote a range inside that band. Solo operator doing under $10K/mo lands in Starter ($1.5K–$3K setup, $197–$397/mo). Small team doing $10–30K/mo lands in Grow ($3K–$7.5K setup, $397–$697/mo). Multi-truck doing $30–100K/mo lands in Scale ($7.5K–$15K setup, $697–$1,497/mo). Multi-location doing $100K+/mo is custom. How fast do you actually deliver?
Most builds ship in 48 hours to 7 days. Multi-location enterprise builds are 2-4 weeks depending on scope. Most agencies take 6-8 weeks. We don't, because we don't run "discovery" calls for a month before touching code.
Is it really all available in Spanish?
Yes. Sabino is natively bilingual (Hispanic-American, raised in SoCal). Chatbots, ads, websites, sales calls, support — all in Spanish if you want it. We are the only SoCal AI agency with a fully Spanish site and pricing published in Spanish. Ver en español →
Do I get locked into a contract?
No. Websites are one-time payments — you own the site. Enterprise monthly services are month-to-month, cancel with 30 days notice. We'd rather earn next month than trap you.
What if I don't love the initial build?
7-day money-back on the setup fee if the first build isn't what we agreed to. Enterprise monthly fees start only after you approve the live site.
Why is your range better value than other agencies?
Two reasons: (1) AI-native workflow — one builder does what a 5-person team does at WebFX or Thrive; (2) no bloated office/sales department. We pass the margin through, not pocket it. Plus we build our own voice AI + dispatch + dashboards — competitors rent Vapi, Voiceflow, and HighLevel and charge you for the markup.
Do you service my specific industry?
Almost certainly. 40+ live demos including contractors, HVAC, plumbing, dental, medical, auto services, detailing, real estate, restaurants, legal, fitness, med spa, e-commerce, and more. Browse demos →
Where are you based? Do you work in person?
Orange County, California (based in Fullerton, CSUF). We serve all of SoCal in person — OC, LA, Inland Empire, San Diego. Remote clients served nationwide. In-person closes still convert 5-10x better, so we'll meet at your shop if you're local.
What payment methods do you accept?
Stripe (card, ACH, Apple/Google Pay), Zelle, and wire. Mercury business checking for ACH. We'll invoice or send a Stripe link — whichever is easier. 50/50 or 40/30/30 payment split available on setup fees.
