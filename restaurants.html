Restaurant FAQ

Can I actually take online orders without DoorDash?
Yes. Rivven integrates direct-ordering tools (ChowNow, Toast online, Square online, or a custom Stripe checkout). You keep 95%+ of the order vs 70% with third-party portals. Biggest margin win in restaurant tech.
What about POS integration?
Enterprise supports Toast, Square, Clover, Lightspeed integrations. Orders flow straight to the kitchen printer. Growth tier keeps it simpler — orders land in email/SMS/dashboard to avoid POS complexity.
Do you handle reservations too?
Yes. We can build a direct reservation flow or integrate OpenTable/Resy/SevenRooms. AI chatbot confirms by text, reduces no-shows with reminder SMS.
¿Pueden hacer el sitio en español?
Sí. El sitio completo, el menú, el sistema de pedidos y el chatbot — todo en español. Sabino es bilingüe nativo. Casi ningún restaurante en SoCal lo hace bien. Es una ventaja gigante sin usar.
Can you help us get more Google reviews?
Yes. Growth tier includes an SMS review engine. After a customer pays or picks up, they get a text link to leave a Google review. Average lift: 3-5x review volume in 60 days.
